PHOTOS
======

This folder holds the original photo files for the website. Choose which photo goes where in the admin
(fuyamo.co.za/admin): Photos, FAQs & reviews → Photos, and each trip under Trips. New photos you upload
there are saved here, and the build makes web-sized copies in site/assets/img/.

The subfolders (headers, trips, moments, …) hold the photos the site started with. spare/ isn't used on
the site; it's a place to keep photos you might use later.
