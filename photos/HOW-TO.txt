PHOTOS
======

This folder holds the original photo files for the website. Choose which photo goes where in the admin
(fuyamo.co.za/admin): Photos, FAQs & reviews → Photos, and each trip under Trips. New photos you upload
there are saved here, and the build makes web-sized copies in site/assets/img/.

Each photo is used in one place only. A trip's own photos (trips/<trip>/) also show on that trip's cards.
The admin's photo picker marks photos already in use as "Used". uploads/ holds everything added in the
admin, and headers/home.jpg is the home page photo. spare/ isn't used on the site; it's a place to keep
photos you might use later.
