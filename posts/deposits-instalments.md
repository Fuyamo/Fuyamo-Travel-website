The full price of a trip is the biggest thing that stops people from travelling. But you don't need it all upfront. With Fuyamo, a deposit holds your seat, and you pay the rest in instalments before you go, with no interest. Think of it as a lay-by for travel. Here's exactly how it works, what it costs on our 2027 trips, and how to make saving for a holiday easier.

## How paying for a Fuyamo trip works

### Step 1: Pay a deposit to reserve your seat

Deposits start at R2,500 per person and depend on the trip. You can pay on Yamo, our booking app, or by EFT to our bank account. If you pay by EFT, use your name and the trip as the reference (for example "Thandi Bali") and send your proof of payment to info@fuyamo.co.za or on WhatsApp. Once we receive it, we confirm your seat on WhatsApp or by email.

### Step 2: Pay the balance your way

There's no interest, and you choose how to pay:

- **Monthly (our recommendation).** Split the balance into equal payments over the months before you travel. It's the easiest way to stay on track.
- **At your own pace.** Top up any amount, any time, whenever you have money to spare.
- **In full.** Pay once and you have nothing to think about until you pack.

### Step 3: Settle the balance 30 days before you fly

Your trip must be fully paid 30 days before departure. If you book less than 30 days before a trip leaves, you pay the full amount when you book.

The full steps and our banking details are on [How to book](/how-to-book/).

## Travel now, pay later, or pay before you go?

"Travel now, pay later" usually means credit: a personal loan, a credit card or a buy-now-pay-later plan. You travel first, then keep paying for months afterwards, usually with interest and fees on top.

Our instalments work the other way around, like a lay-by. You pay the trip off before you travel, so:

- **There's no interest.** The trip price is the trip price.
- **There's no debt when you get home.** Your holiday is paid for before you board.
- **You budget with a deadline.** Knowing your trip date makes it much easier to stick to the plan.

## Paying on Yamo or by EFT

**On Yamo**, you can pay your deposit and instalments by card or EFT, see what you've paid and what's still due, and keep your vouchers, trip documents and indemnity forms in one place. Find our trips on [Yamo](/yamo/).

**By EFT**, you pay straight into our bank account. Always use the same reference (your name and the trip), and send proof of every payment to info@fuyamo.co.za or on WhatsApp, so we can match it to your booking.

Booking with friends? Each traveller pays their own deposit and instalments under their own name, so everyone's payments stay clear.

## Why is the deposit non-refundable?

Your deposit isn't a booking fee. It's used to secure flights, accommodation and activity bookings in your name, often months in advance, and those suppliers don't give that money back. That's why deposits are non-refundable.

To protect yourself, take out travel insurance with cancellation cover as soon as you book. If something serious happens before you travel, such as an illness, a good policy can cover the costs you can't get back. Read the policy wording to see what's included.

## How to save for a holiday: tips that work

- **Pick the trip and the date first.** A real deadline is the best motivation there is.
- **Pay yourself on payday.** Set up a monthly transfer for the day your salary lands, before the money gets spent.
- **Use a separate savings pocket.** Most South African banks let you open a savings pocket or second account in a few taps. Out of sight, out of mind.
- **Put windfalls to work.** A bonus, a 13th cheque, a SARS refund or birthday money can clear a big chunk of your balance at once.
- **Swap one habit.** Redirect one subscription, a few takeaways or a weekly coffee run into your trip for a few months.
- **Save for spending money too.** Your trip price covers what's listed under "What's included" for your trip. Budget separately for meals that aren't included, optional activities, visas or levies, travel insurance and shopping.

## Instalment questions we get asked

### Can I pay for a holiday in instalments?

Yes. Pay a deposit to reserve your seat, then pay the balance monthly, at your own pace or in full. It must be settled 30 days before departure.

### Do you charge interest on instalments?

No. You pay the trip price and nothing more.

### Is the deposit refundable?

No. Deposits are non-refundable, because they secure flights, accommodation and activities on your behalf.

### Can I pay off my trip early?

Yes. You can top up any amount at any time or pay the full balance whenever you like.

### Can I pay by card?

Yes, on Yamo. You can also pay by EFT into our bank account.

### How do I know my payment has been received?

Send your proof of payment to info@fuyamo.co.za or on WhatsApp, and we'll confirm it. On Yamo, your payments show up in the app.
