## A deposit holds your seat

Deposits start at R2,500 per person, depending on the trip. They are non-refundable because they secure flights and bookings for you.

## Pay the balance in instalments

Spread the rest over the months before your trip. The full balance must be settled 30 days before departure.

## Use the right reference

Your name plus the trip name, for example “Thandi Bali”. Then send proof of payment to info@fuyamo.co.za or WhatsApp. The full steps are on [How to book](/how-to-book/).

## Birthday month bonus

Travelling in your birthday month? You get R500 off.
