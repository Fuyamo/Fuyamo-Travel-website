MTN Bushfire is one of Africa's best-loved music festivals: three days of music, art and dancing under the stars in the hills of Eswatini. ## What is Bushfire?

Bushfire has grown from a small gathering into one of the biggest festivals in southern Africa. The 2026 edition brought more than 23,000 festivalgoers, who call themselves Fire Starters, from over 55 countries, and it was the festival's 14th sold-out year in a row.

The line-up mixes African and international artists across many genres. Beyond the main stage, there's a craft and design market, food from across the region, art installations and workshops. The venue itself, House on Fire, is a quirky open-air arts venue full of sculptures and mosaics, set among the green hills of the Malkerns Valley.

## Getting to Bushfire from Johannesburg

Malkerns is about 370 km from Johannesburg. The drive takes about 4 to 5 hours through Mpumalanga to the Oshoek border post (Ngwenya on the Eswatini side), then on past Mbabane into the Malkerns Valley.

Festival weekend is a very busy time at Oshoek, and the organisers warn about heavy traffic, so expect queues at the border. That's one of the best reasons to travel with a group: you sit back while we handle the road, the parking and the logistics.

## Do South Africans need a passport for Eswatini?

Yes. Eswatini is a separate country, so you need a valid passport to cross the border. Your South African ID card or ID book isn't enough. You don't need a visa.

- Check your passport is valid and has blank pages for the entry and exit stamps.
- Keep it on you, not packed in your bag in the boot.
- Under-18s have to travel with the right documents. Our [passport checklist for South Africans](/travel-talk/passport-checklist/) explains what's needed.

## Money at Bushfire

The lilangeni is pegged to the rand, so prices are the same in either currency, and rand is accepted all over Eswatini.

At the festival itself, the 2026 edition was **fully cashless**: you paid for food, drinks and merchandise with an NFC wristband loaded with money. Expect the same in 2027, and load enough to cover food and drinks for the weekend. Carry a bank card and a little cash for the road and the craft shops outside the festival.

## Camping at Bushfire

Camping is part of the Bushfire experience. You're close to the music, you meet people from all over the world, and there's no driving at night. Our trip includes 3 nights of camping, breakfast every morning and one group dinner.

Late May in the Malkerns Valley means warm, sunny days and cold nights, so the most important thing you'll pack is something warm to sleep in.

### What to pack for Bushfire

- Your passport
- A warm sleeping bag and a pillow
- Warm layers for the night: a hoodie, a jacket, a beanie and thick socks
- Comfortable shoes you can dance in all day, and that can handle dust or mud
- A rain jacket or poncho, just in case
- Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat
- A headlamp or small torch for the campsite
- A power bank and charging cable
- Earplugs and an eye mask, if you want to sleep at a festival
- Toiletries, wet wipes, toilet paper and hand sanitiser
- A refillable water bottle
- A small backpack or bum bag for the arena
- Any medication you need

### What to leave at home

- Valuables you don't need, like expensive jewellery
- Glass bottles
- Anything you'd be sad to lose in the dust

## Tips for your first Bushfire

- **Pick a meeting point** with your friends in case phones die or the signal drops.
- **Drink water** between drinks, especially in the afternoon sun.
- **Pace yourself.** It's three days, not one night.
- **Explore beyond the main stage.** The market, the art and the smaller stages are half the fun.
- **Look after each other.** Travel as a group, leave as a group.
- **Leave no trace.** Take your rubbish to the bins and leave the campsite as you found it.

## Eswatini beyond the festival

The Malkerns Valley is also known for its crafts. Swazi Candles and Gone Rural, which sells handmade baskets and homeware made by women in rural Eswatini, are both nearby and make great gifts. Mlilwane Wildlife Sanctuary, a short drive away, is a peaceful place for walks and cycling among zebra and antelope.
