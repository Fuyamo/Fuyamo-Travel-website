## We drive, you relax

Road transport from Johannesburg is included, so there are no flights and no car hire to sort out.

## Two nights in Maputo

A city tour and South Beach. Expect seafood, music and a city that stays up late.

## Two nights in Bilene

A calm lagoon with a boat cruise to Coco Island. Add canoeing, jet skis or beach horse riding if you like.

## Have your documents ready

You'll cross a border, so keep your passport handy. Ask us what you need before you travel.
