## Ubud: jungle, temples and the Swing

The first half is green and slow. You'll do the Ubud cultural tour, a waterfall, the Bali Swing and a day pass at Cretya Ubud.

## The Nusa Penida day

A full day out to the island for cliffs and viewpoints. It's an early start and worth every minute.

## Uluwatu at sunset

The clifftop temple is one of the most photographed spots in Bali. Bring something to cover your shoulders and knees.

## Canggu: the beach half

The last two nights are beach clubs, cafés and time to do nothing at all.
