## Check the expiry date first

Many countries want your passport valid for months beyond your return date. If yours is close, renew it before anything else. Home Affairs queues don't care about your flight.

## Count your blank pages

Stamps need space. Multi-country trips like our [3 Countries tour](/trips/vicfalls/) can use several pages in one week.

## Ask us what your destination requires

Entry rules change, and they differ by passport. Visa and passport assistance is part of what we do, so WhatsApp us and we'll tell you exactly what you need for your trip.

## Keep copies

Save a photo of your passport and visa on your phone and in your email, and pack one printed copy separately from the original.
