## Nungwi: three nights of beach

On the northern tip of the island. Swim with turtles, take the sunset dhow cruise and go island hopping to Prison Island and Nakupenda.

## Stone Town: two nights of history

Narrow streets, carved doors and the historic landmarks tour. Great for markets and food.

## Worth adding

Mnemba snorkelling and swimming with dolphins, a clear kayak or a jet ski. All optional, at your own cost.
