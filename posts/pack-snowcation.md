## Layers, not one big jacket

A thermal base layer, a warm fleece or jersey, and a waterproof outer layer you can zip up or open as the day changes.

## Waterproof gloves and warm socks

Wet hands end a ski day fast. Bring a spare pair of socks for the drive home.

## Sunglasses and sunscreen

Snow reflects sunlight. You will burn faster on the slopes than on a beach.

## Something for the evening

After the slopes it's all about getting warm. A beanie, comfy shoes and a power bank for the photos.

## Book your spot

Dates and prices are on the [Lesotho Snowcation](/trips/lesotho/) page, and there’s a [Family Edition](/trips/lesothofam/) for travelling with kids.
